# Riverside assets

`qwantani_sunrise_puresky_1k.hdr` is a 1024 × 512 equirectangular Radiance RGBE environment from Poly Haven. Greg Zaal photographed it and Jarod Guest processed the sky-only version. [Asset page](https://polyhaven.com/a/qwantani_sunrise_puresky)

The file is Poly Haven’s original 1K HDR download (1,033,946 bytes). It was downloaded directly and copied without conversion. Its MD5 is `9803c0c384d5175805c28f83b8489338`. [Exact download](https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/1k/qwantani_sunrise_puresky_1k.hdr)

The HDRI is available under CC0 1.0. [Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The alpine scene uses Qwantani Dusk 2 (Pure Sky), photographed by Greg Zaal and processed by Jarod Guest, retrieved at 1K in Radiance HDR format. The original file is unchanged (MD5 d400530e33f683654e70b4a087b6fe8d). [Artist and CC0 asset](https://polyhaven.com/a/qwantani_dusk_2_puresky) · [Exact download](https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/1k/qwantani_dusk_2_puresky_1k.hdr).

The plank grain used on barn siding, timber and fences is a derivative of Dimitrios Savva’s CC0 Weathered Plank Siding. The 1K diffuse JPEG (MD5 90f818fd61602ac7268ee89857e77396) was desaturated, contrast-normalized (2% cutoff) and resized to 128 × 128. The live material tiles this lightweight image over the painted boards. [Artist and asset](https://polyhaven.com/a/weathered_plank_siding) · [Original diffuse map](https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/weathered_plank_siding/weathered_plank_siding_diff_1k.jpg).

The scene geometry, vehicle and animal designs were generated for this study. Minecraft trailers were visual references only. The web renderer bundles Three.js under its [MIT license](THREE-LICENSE.txt).

Composition references researched: [Minecraft’s Relaxing Farm Morning](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xLo-BrCh7JQ), [Relaxing Fireplace](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FqnAV6tkwzA), [Relaxing Cherry Grove](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EjBdURAwJHI), and [Drift Away Ambience](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJ6Lbsmb1lY).
